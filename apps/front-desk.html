How it works

Set up once. Picks up every time.

1We learn your business

A short interview about your hours, services, service area and what counts as an emergency. You review every word it's allowed to say.

2It answers when you can't

Calls forward to Front Desk when you're busy or after hours. You keep your number.

3You get the job

Each call becomes a written job request with the details read back and confirmed. Emergencies get flagged right away.

Honest by design

These rules are built in for every business, and no setting can turn them off.

✕
Pretend to be human

✕

✕

✕
