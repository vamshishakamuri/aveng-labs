After you download

Super Dog isn't code-signed yet, so your computer asks once before opening it.

macOS

Open the .dmg (Apple Silicon or Intel), drag Super Dog to Applications and open it. If macOS warns you: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Windows

Run the .exe, no admin needed. If SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.

Linux

Use the .AppImage or .deb. On GNOME the tray icon needs the AppIndicator extension.

Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $4.99 once and it's yours. No account, no ads.

How do I get the app after paying?

Right after checkout you get a download page and an email with links to the installers for every system, plus your license key.

How do I activate Super Dog?

The first time it opens, paste the license key from your receipt email. Super Dog checks it with Lemon Squeezy once to activate, then works offline. Each key works on 3 computers; to move to a new one, choose Deactivate this computer… in the tray menu on the old one.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The app isn't code-signed yet, so macOS and Windows show a one-time warning. Follow the note under your download and you won't see it again.

Will it get in the way while I work?

Never. Every click goes straight through the pet, it never steals focus, and it keeps out from under your pointer.

Can I change how often it reminds me?

Yes. Open the tray menu to change the reminder interval, switch pets, or pause reminders.

Does it start when my computer starts?

If you want. Turn on Start at login (macOS) or Start with Windows in the tray menu.
