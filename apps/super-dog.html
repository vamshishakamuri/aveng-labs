Questions

Is Super Dog really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The app isn't code-signed yet, so macOS and Windows show a one-time warning. Follow the note under your download and you won't see it again.

Will it get in the way while I work?

Never. Every click goes straight through the pet, it never steals focus, and it keeps out from under your pointer.

Can I change how often it reminds me?

Yes — open the tray menu to change the reminder interval, switch pets, or pause reminders.

Does it start when my computer starts?

If you want. Turn on Start at login (macOS) or Start with Windows in the tray menu.
