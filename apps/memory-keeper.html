Memory Keeper · A family storybook · Coming soon

They tell the stories. Memory Keeper makes the book.

Memory Keeper is an AI interviewer for family stories. It asks your mom, dad or grandparent a question out loud, listens, and follows up the way a curious grandchild would. Then it writes each chapter in their own words, for a book the family can read, print and listen to.

How it works

One chapter at a time. No typing.

1Pick a chapter

Each chapter covers one part of a life, such as "Growing up" or "Love and marriage". Start wherever the stories are.

2Answer out loud

The question is read aloud in a warm voice. They just talk. The next question builds on what they said.

3Read the book

Each chapter is written in their own first-person voice. Print it, save it as a PDF, or read it on screen and play back the original recordings.

Honest by design

What it will never do.

A family book is only worth keeping if it's true. Memory Keeper writes down what was said and stops there.

✕
Invent a name, a date or an event

Chapters are written only from what the storyteller said. If they didn't say it, it isn't in the book.

✕
Tell the story for them

Every chapter is in the first person, built from their own answers.

✕
Leave their voice behind

The on-screen book plays the original recording of each answer, so you can hear them tell it.

✕
Make them type

Questions are read aloud and answers are spoken.

Questions

Good questions.

Can I use it today?

Not yet. A working version runs on our own computers, but it isn't open to the public. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email when it is.

Who is it for?

A parent or grandparent with stories worth keeping. It's usually a gift from a grown child, and it's made for people who don't enjoy technology.

Do they need a computer?

Today, yes: the interview runs in a web browser with a microphone. We're building interviews over an ordinary phone call so they won't need one. That part isn't finished.

Is a person asking the questions?

No. The questions are asked and the chapters are written by AI. It works only from what the storyteller said and doesn't add names, dates or events.

Can I order a printed book?

Not yet. Today the finished book prints cleanly on your own printer or saves as a PDF.

The price isn't set yet. It will be a one-time gift purchase, and people on the waitlist will hear first.
