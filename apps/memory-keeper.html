Memory Keeper · A family storybook · Coming soon

Ammamma answered the phone. A week later, the family read her chapter.

Kiran lives an ocean away from his grandmother. He calls on Sundays, and there is never time for the old stories.

One afternoon her phone rings. She hears Kiran's own voice say hello. Then a gentle voice tells her, in Telugu, that her grandson asked it to call, that it is an automated helper and not a person, and that it will write down what she says.

It asks about the house she grew up in. She tells it about the well in the courtyard. It asks who drew the water. She talks until her tea goes cold.

A week later the family opens her chapter. It is in Telugu, with English beside it, in her own words. Nothing has been added.

This is an illustration of how Memory Keeper works. Kiran and Ammamma are fictional.

How it works

They pick up the phone. That's all they do.

1Tell us who to call

Give their name and number, and record a hello in your own voice.

2We ring their phone

An ordinary phone call. No app, no screen and no typing for them.

3It asks, and listens

It says who asked for the call, then asks gentle follow-up questions based on what they just said.

4You read the chapter

Their answers become a chapter in a book, in their own words. It prints or saves as a PDF.

EnglishTeluguHindi coming next

English and Telugu have been tried on real calls. A Telugu call gives you the chapter in Telugu with an English version beside it.

Honest by design

What we promise.

These are someone's memories. You should always know what happens to them.

✓
It says what it is

The call says plainly that it is an automated helper, not a person, and that answers are written down. They can stop at any time by pressing star.

✓
It never invents

The chapter uses only what they said. Anything it wasn't sure it heard correctly is flagged for you to check, and left out of the printed book.

✓
Your family sees only its own stories

Each family has its own private account.

✓
See everything we hold

A "Your data" page lists everything Memory Keeper holds for your family.

✓
Download it, any time

All of it as one file: every question and answer, each book as a page, and recordings where they exist.

✓
Delete it, any time

Delete one story, or delete everything and close the account. Deleting removes every copy Memory Keeper itself holds.

What deleting can't reach

Two other companies handle what is said. Twilio carries the phone call. Anthropic, the maker of Claude, receives the answers as text so the questions and the chapter can be written. Each keeps what passes through it for a limited time under its own policy, and deleting in Memory Keeper cannot reach into their systems. Anthropic's commercial terms say it does not train its models on this content. Read their own words: Twilio's privacy page and Anthropic's privacy page.

Questions

Good questions.

Can I use it today?

Not yet. It is still being tested and isn't open to the public. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email when it is.

Do they need a smartphone or a computer?

No. The call goes to an ordinary phone. There is no app, no screen and no typing for them.

Is a person asking the questions?

No. It is an automated helper, and it says so at the start of the call. It also says who asked for the call.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Telugu have been tried on real calls. Hindi is coming next.

What if it mishears something?

Answers it wasn't sure it heard correctly are flagged for the family to check, and they are left out of the printed book.
