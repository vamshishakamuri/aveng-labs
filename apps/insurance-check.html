How it works

Set up once. Checks every night.

1Connect Open Dental

You switch on our key in Open Dental. We read your upcoming appointments and the plan on file for each patient.

2We check overnight

For each patient, an electronic eligibility check goes to the payer through a clearinghouse, dated to the visit.

3Your desk reads the answer

A benefits breakdown lands in the patient's chart, active coverage is marked verified, and anything unclear goes on a short list.

Honest by design

Questions

Good questions.

Which practice software does it work with?

Open Dental, for now. It reads appointments and plans through Open Dental's API and writes the results back into the patient's chart.

Does it replace calling the insurance company?

For many patients, yes: the electronic answer covers active coverage, maximums, deductibles and what the plan pays. Some payers send very little, and network status is never in an electronic answer. Those cases go on your list for a person.

What about patients booked by phone with no plan on file?

If they gave their insurance on the call, it's checked from what they said, and your list tells you to add the plan in Open Dental.

Is patient information protected?

We sign a Business Associate Agreement with your practice before any patient data is shared, and the morning list carries patient numbers, not names.
